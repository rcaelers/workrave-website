Open **Preferences → Timers → Monitoring** to configure how Workrave detects keyboard and mouse activity.

![Preferences - Timer - Monitoring](/images/screenshots/1.12/preferences-monitoring.png#center)

- Use alternate monitor

  In some rare cases Workrave may fail to detect your activity.
  In this case you can enable this option to instruct Workrave to use a different way of monitoring your activity.

- Mouse sensitivity

  Workrave tries to ignore small movements of the mouse pointer.
  The higher this value, the lower the sensitivity.
  This setting appears when **Use alternate monitor** is enabled.

## Focus Assist (Windows)

When the Windows Focus Assist integration is included, enable **Set operation mode based on Windows Focus Assist** and choose **Quiet** or **Suspended** to use that [operation mode](/docs/1.12/menus/operation-mode/) while Focus Assist is active.
