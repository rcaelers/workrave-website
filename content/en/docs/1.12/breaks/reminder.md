title: Break reminder
---

When it is time to take a break, Workrave will first gently announce the break by
means of a small non-intrusive window. This window will automatically move out
of the way if you choose to ignore it, for example to finish your current
activity.

The examples show **Classic** and **Sanctuary** appearances. Select **Use Sanctuary UI** in [Preferences → User interface → General](/docs/1.12/settings/ui/#general) to switch between them. Select an image to view it at full size.

{{< ui-comparison alt="Microbreak prelude window" classic="/images/screenshots/1.12/prelude-microbreak.png" sanctuary="/images/screenshots/1.12/prelude-microbreak-sanctuary.png" >}}

After about 20 seconds of ignoring the break reminder, the window will start
flashing orange.

{{< ui-comparison alt="Microbreak prelude window" classic="/images/screenshots/1.12/prelude-microbreak-warn.png" sanctuary="/images/screenshots/1.12/prelude-microbreak-warn-sanctuary.png" >}}

A few seconds later it will start flashing red. If you still continue to
ignore it, the break reminder window will disappear. Depending on your settings
and the number of times you ignored the break, Workrave will either force you to
take the break, or try again in a few minutes.

{{< ui-comparison alt="Microbreak prelude window" classic="/images/screenshots/1.12/prelude-microbreak-warn-red.png" sanctuary="/images/screenshots/1.12/prelude-microbreak-warn-red-sanctuary.png" >}}
